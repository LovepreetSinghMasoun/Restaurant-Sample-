The seam, not the blend

Philosophy

We don't smooth two cuisines into one. We let you taste the exact point where miso meets aji amarillo.

Every dish keeps its two halves legible — a technique from one tradition applied to an ingredient from the other, plated so you can taste the handoff, not just the result.

Our chefs trained across both kitchens before opening this one, and the menu changes with what's in season on either coast.
